What is DummyTrader?

DummyTrader is a free, browser-based paper-trading simulator built for people learning the Indian stock market. "Paper trading" means placing trades with fake money against genuine price movements — a risk-free rehearsal of the real thing. Everything in the app is simulated: the cash is virtual and nothing you do can cost or earn real money. What's real is the mechanics. Order types, margin, leverage, option premiums and expiry all behave the way they do on a live trading platform, so the habits and intuition you build here carry straight over to real trading.

It's designed to be instant and open. There's no mandatory signup and no time-limited demo — just open the simulator, search for a stock or index, and place your first practice trade. Blow up the account, reset, and try again as often as you like. That freedom to fail safely is exactly where the learning happens.

How it works

  1. Open the simulator. You start with a virtual cash balance — no deposit, no card, no signup needed.
  2. Pick an instrument. Search for a stock, or choose an index like Nifty 50 or Bank Nifty and browse its live option chain.
  3. Place a trade. Buy or sell, set quantity, choose a market or limit order — just like a real broker terminal.
  4. Watch your P&L move. Your positions and net worth update in real time as prices change, adjusted for leverage and margin.
  5. Learn and repeat. Review what worked, read the matching guide, and refine your approach — all with zero financial risk.

Why practise with a simulator?

  • Zero risk. Make every beginner mistake without losing money.
  • Learn the mechanics. Understand orders, margin, leverage and option pricing by doing, not just reading.
  • Test strategies. Try an idea over days or weeks before committing real capital.
  • Build discipline. Practise stop-losses and position sizing until they become automatic.
  • No pressure. No signup wall, no expiry, no salesperson — just a sandbox to explore.
Remember: simulated results aren't a promise of real-world performance. Real trading adds costs, taxes, slippage and — most of all — emotion. DummyTrader is an educational tool, not investment advice. Never trade real money you can't afford to lose.

Frequently asked questions

Is DummyTrader really free?

Yes, completely. You trade with virtual money, so there's nothing to pay and nothing to lose.

Is this real-money trading?

No. Every trade uses virtual cash against real, delayed market prices. You can't lose or earn real money — it's a practice simulator for learning only.

Do I need to create an account?

No. You can open the simulator and place your first trade without signing up. Signing in (optional) just lets you save progress.

What can I trade?

Stocks, index futures, and options — including Nifty and Bank Nifty option chains — with realistic prices and margin behaviour.

Can paper trading actually help me improve?

Yes. It lets you learn order types, leverage and risk management with zero financial risk, so you make your beginner mistakes safely. It can't replicate the emotion of real money, but it builds the foundation.

Is DummyTrader investment advice?

No. Nothing on this site is investment advice or a recommendation to buy or sell any security. It's an educational simulator. For real-money decisions, consult a licensed financial adviser.
